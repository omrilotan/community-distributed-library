How it works Start one

Connect your assistant

Every library has its own MCP server. Add it to Claude, ChatGPT or any assistant that speaks MCP, sign in once, and the assistant can search the shelf, list what you own and arrange loans for you. It takes about a minute.

  1. Add your library

    In your library, open My account, then Connect an assistant. It shows your library's MCP address, such as https://mcp.our-street.example.org/mcp, and how to add it to each assistant.

    In Claude: Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector, then paste the address.

  2. Sign in

    Your assistant opens your library's sign-in page in a window of its own. Enter the email you use for the library, and ask for a sign-in link.

  3. Type the code

    The email has a link and an eight-digit code. A link opens in your phone's browser or mail app, not in the assistant's window, so type the code there instead.

    The code works only in the window that asked for it, and only once.

  4. Choose what it may do

    The library asks what the assistant may do. Looking is ticked; acting for you, like asking for a book or answering a request, is yours to tick. Allow, and you are back in your assistant.

Then just ask

In your own words, the way you would ask a neighbour. It checks with you before it writes to anyone.

  • “Is anyone lending a ladder this weekend?”

    It searches the shelf and offers to ask the owner.

  • “Add these three books to my shelf.”

    With a photo of their spines, or just their titles.

  • “Accept Noa's request and say Saturday morning suits me.”

    It answers the request and writes to the borrower.

  • Change your mind any time

    Your connected assistants are listed under My account, each with a button to disconnect it. An assistant never sees other members' addresses or emails.
