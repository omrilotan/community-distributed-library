Run your own library

A lending library for your street takes about twenty minutes to open, costs US$5 a month and needs no programming. Here it is in a minute.

Read what it says

Somewhere on your street, there's a drill you need once a year, and a neighbour who already owns one.

Community Distributed Library turns that street into a lending library. No building, no central stock: everything stays on its owner's shelf until someone asks.

You can open one for your community. Pick a name, the middle of your neighbourhood, and whether it lends books or things. It runs on a domain you own, in your own Cloudflare account, for five dollars a month, email included.

No programming needed. Open the code in Claude Code and type “open a library”. It walks you through the one key it needs, builds the library, and makes you its first admin.

Give it a description, its languages and a colour. Then invite your first neighbours. It's invite-only, so everyone who joins is vouched for by someone already in.

From there, the library runs itself. Members lend from their own shelves, agree a time, and confirm each handoff at the door with a short code. Reminders chase late returns, so you don't have to.

Ask a neighbour or two to moderate: tags, duplicates, reports. About an hour a week.

No money changes hands, nothing ships, and nobody sits in between. The members and the data stay yours, and the code is public domain. Start one for your street at community-distributed-library.com.

What you need

  • A domain on Cloudflare

    The library lives at a name under it, such as our-street.example.org.

  • The Workers Paid plan

    US$5 a month on your own Cloudflare account. It covers the library's email too: 3,000 a month, then US$0.35 per 1,000.

  • About twenty minutes

    Once, to open it. After that it runs itself.

Two ways to open one

  • Let Claude Code do it

    Open the code in Claude Code and type /open-a-library. It walks you through the one key it needs, builds the library and makes you its first admin. Your key goes into a file on your computer, never into the chat.

  • Follow the guide

    Make a Cloudflare token, choose a name, an address, the middle of your neighbourhood and whether it lends books or things, and run one command. The self-hosting guide has every step.

After that

  • Make it yours

    Sign in with your email. In Moderation › Settings, give it a description, its languages and a colour.

  • Invite your first neighbours

    By email, or with a link to share in your street's group. It is invite-only, so everyone who joins is vouched for by someone already in.

  • A neighbour or two to moderate

    Tags, duplicates, reports: about an hour a week. Members lend, agree times and hand over on their own, and reminders chase late returns.
