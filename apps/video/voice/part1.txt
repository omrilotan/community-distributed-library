Somewhere on your street, there's a drill you need once a year, and a neighbour who already owns one.

Community Distributed Library turns that street into a lending library. No building, no central stock: everything stays on its owner's shelf until someone asks.

You can open one for your community. Pick a name, the middle of your neighbourhood, and whether it lends books or things. It runs on a domain you own, in your own Cloudflare account, for five dollars a month, email included.

No programming needed. Open the code in Claude Code and type "open a library". It walks you through the one key it needs, builds the library, and makes you its first admin.
