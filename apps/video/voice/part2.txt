Give it a description, its languages and a colour. Then invite your first neighbours. It's invite-only, so everyone who joins is vouched for by someone already in.

From there, the library runs itself. Members lend from their own shelves, agree a time, and confirm each handoff at the door with a short code. Reminders chase late returns, so you don't have to.

Ask a neighbour or two to moderate: tags, duplicates, reports. About an hour a week.

No money changes hands, nothing ships, and nobody sits in between. The members and the data stay yours, and the code is public domain. Start one for your street at community-distributed-library dot com.
